What it does
  • Headline says "match" or "don't match", never "good deal" or "bad deal". The gaps carry the judgement.
  • Three stripes, three dollar figures, each with its comparison spelled out. Nothing out of 100.
  • The breakdown is the number the finance manager can't dispute: it's their own payment reversed.
  • "Ask for" chips are copy-ready scripts. "I need 10 minutes" copies a text to send to a partner.
  • Decided: thresholds scale with the deal. A gap over 3% of implied all-in is amber, over 8% is red. On this $34,881 example that's $1,046 and $2,790. On a $12,000 used car it's $360 and $960, which is still worth asking about.
